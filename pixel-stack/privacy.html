PIXEL STACK(이하 "앱")은 이용자의 개인정보를 수집하지 않습니다. 회원가입이나 로그인이 없으며, 이름·이메일·전화번호·위치·기기 식별자 등 어떤 정보도 요청하거나 수집하지 않습니다.

게임 기록(쌓은 층수, PERFECT 횟수, 최대 연속 PERFECT, 기록한 날짜와 시각, 플레이 횟수)과 소리 켜기/끄기 설정은 이용자의 기기 안에만 저장됩니다. 이 정보는 개발자나 외부 서버로 전송되지 않으며, 앱을 삭제하면 함께 삭제됩니다.

앱에는 광고, 분석(통계), 추적 도구가 포함되어 있지 않으며, 어떤 정보도 제3자에게 제공하지 않습니다.

PIXEL STACK Privacy Policy

Developer: ppongkkong · Effective date: October 2, 2026

1. Information we collect

PIXEL STACK (the "App") does not collect any personal information. There is no sign-up or login, and the App does not request or collect your name, email, phone number, location, device identifiers, or any other data.

2. Data stored on your device

Game records (floors reached, PERFECT count, longest PERFECT streak, the date and time they were set, and number of plays) and your sound on/off setting are stored only on your device. This data is never sent to the developer or any server, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Third parties

The App contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking tools, and shares no information with third parties.

4. Permissions

  • Vibration: used for short haptic feedback when you drop a block or the game ends.

5. Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, when online rankings are added), the changes and the new effective date will be posted on this page.
