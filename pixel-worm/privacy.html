PIXEL WORM Privacy Policy

Developer: ppongkkong · Effective date: October 1, 2026

1. Information we collect

PIXEL WORM (the "App") does not collect any personal information. There is no sign-up or login, and the App does not request or collect your name, email, phone number, location, device identifiers, or any other data.

2. Data stored on your device

Game records (scores, the date and time they were set, number of plays, and apples eaten) are stored only on your device. They are never sent to the developer or any server, and they are deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Third parties

The App contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking tools, and shares no information with third parties.

4. Permissions

  • Vibration: used for short haptic feedback when you change direction or eat an apple.

5. Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, when online rankings are added), the changes and the new effective date will be posted on this page.
